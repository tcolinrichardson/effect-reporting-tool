Formulas reference
Likelihood: β̂ ~ Normal(θ, SE²)
Prior: θ ~ Normal(0, τ²), where τ → ∞ approximates flat
Posterior: θ | data ~ Normal(μpost, σ²post)
with σ²_post = 1 / (1/τ² + 1/SE²) and μ_post = σ²_post · (β̂ / SE²)
P(θ > t): 1 − Φ((t − μpost) / σpost)
MDE @ 80% power: (zα/2 + z1−β) · SE ≈ 2.80 · SE
Under a flat prior, posterior mean = β̂ and posterior SD = SE, so credible interval = confidence interval and P(θ > 0) = 1 − (one-sided p-value).
